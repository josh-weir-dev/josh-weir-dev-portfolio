Spiderman Swinging Reconstruction

A physics-driven swinging traversal system, implemented with Unreal Engine 5.8.

C++ Unreal Engine
View Source on GitHub

Systems Breakdown

  • Sphere Trace & Anchor Point Selection

    • Apporach:

    Use the spherical area around the player to detect potential anchor points for swinging.

    Fixed-Point Swinging

    Attached the player to a fixed point to achieve the swinging motion throughout the air.

    Key Challenge:

    Ensuring the player attaches to a valid anchor point without attaching to the floor and being brought straight down.

  • Swing Trajectory & Velocity

    • Approach:

    Implemented a physics-based system that uses vector math to calculate the velocity needed to maintain a swinging trajectory

    Launch Velocity

    Uses a launch velocity to maintain the player's momentum so they can continue on the swinging motion and not lose momentum when releasing a swing.

    Key Challenge:

    Ensuring the player does not fall too quickly after releasing a swing. This was fixed by applying a launch velocity after release.
