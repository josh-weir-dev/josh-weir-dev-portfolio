Systems Breakdown

  • Procedural Dungeon Generation

    • To achieve the roguelike experience, I implemented procedurally generated dungeons that creates a unique layout of template rooms and corridors.
    I used a grid-based approach with multiple potential starting points and a path-validation check to ensure the layout had a pathway that could lead the player to the final room.
    I had to solve the challenge of ensuring connectivity while maintaining variety in the generated layouts, solving issues such as rooms overwriting themselves and rooms blocking the player from progressing

  • Enemy Spawning

    • To increase the level of variety, I added multiple spawn points for enemies, ensuring a dynamic and challenging experience for the player.
    To keep it balanced, I placed a cap on the amount of enemies that could spawn at one given time as well as addiing a delay between each enemy spawn.
    I had to ensure each spawn point had a valid path to the player at all times so the enemy movement logic was not broken. Furthermore, I had to place these points strategically to ensure the player could not just avoid the enemies completely.
